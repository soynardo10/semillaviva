      Definitions

      Revenue: sales of the quarter (Raíz Crédito: interest income). EBITDA: operating income, since no company reports depreciation or amortization. Net monthly burn: decrease in cash during the quarter divided by three. Runway: cash divided by monthly burn. TVPI: total value divided by capital invested, held at cost. PAR30: loans more than 30 days late as a share of the loan portfolio. DSCR: cash flow available for debt service divided by debt service.
